Privacy Policy

How roxxx.shop handles your information when you use our website and place orders.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when placing an order, such as your name, mobile number, delivery address, and bKash transaction details. We also collect basic usage information needed to keep the website working.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, arrange delivery, verify payments, and communicate with you about your purchases. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment Details

You enter your bKash number and transaction ID directly on our payment page. We do not store your bKash PIN or full wallet credentials. Payment verification is performed manually by our moderator.

4. Local Storage

roxxx.shop uses browser local storage to keep your cart, order history, and delivery information on your device. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

5. Data Retention

We keep your order information for as long as needed to manage your purchases and handle any related support requests.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no online service is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the website means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the Customer Support page to send us a message.
